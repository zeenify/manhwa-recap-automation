  classic per-chapter pipeline is untouched).

## 🔍 Research 🔍

* Study of Leo's Manhwa (4.3k subs, 100k+-view 4–15h videos) →
  `research/leo_manhwa_format_notes.md`: one binge video per story, ~210 WPM
  third-person plot recitation, square-ish art tiles, blank bubbles, no
  captions.
* Frame-by-frame verified shot grammar →
  `research/leo_shot_grammar_findings.md`: one image per narration clause,
  image batches, slide-ins, zoom creep, full-screen top-down pans for
  tall/scenic strips, hard cuts between batches. Supersedes the earlier
  "continuous scroll" misread.
* Crop verification against the actual source manhua ("It Turns Out That They
  Are the Protagonists", native 1080×1080 pages): their "crops" are whole
  source pages with text washed — smart cropping ≠ cropping at all for
  paginated sources; for long-strip series the reader adapts instead.

## 🎞️ Film lane (long-form) 🎞️
### Added

* `tools/assemble_film.py` (new) — batch-model renderer; one script clause =
  one batch of 1/2/3 images: 1 image = the star, big and centered (tall strip
  with h/w ≥ 2.2 promotes to a solo full-screen top-down pan); 2 images =
  balanced left/right halves, no overlap; 3 images = left/middle/right
  thirds. Images slide into their slots staggered by narration progress, zoom
  creep holds, batches disappear when their clause ends — no leftovers.
  Scene-group changes get a white-flash wipe. Narration audio is the clock.
* `agents/reader-agent-film.md` (new) — adaptive smart-crop reader: given the
  crop GOAL (tiles aspect 0.85–1.4 with focal x-windows, pans for continuous
  strips, skips for junk/watermarks/title cards) it inventories the chapter's
  own layout and reasons out its crops per series. Output:
  `film_assets.json`.
* `agents/writer-agent-film.md` (new) — image-first film writer: one flowing
  clause per scheduled image (25–40 words, channel voice at ~210 WPM), batch
  tags `[NN tile]` / `[NN pan]`, scene-group wipes, and a mandatory image
  safety-net (the writer Reads 3–4 pivotal asset PNGs to verify context).
  Caught two real reader mis-descriptions on its first run.
* Test production on return-of-the-top-class-master ch001: 66 tiles + 5 pans
  + 13 scene groups, all aspects in spec, 16 x-window crops, adaptive splits
  where slicing would cut a figure; 70s proof film through the full chain
  (reader → film-writer → per-clause TTS → batch assembler).

### Changed

* Removed during review (wrong vs batch grammar or unneeded): per-slide
  accumulation, endless drift, pop-out tile evictions, weak background blur,
  experimental text-wash (bubbles stay readable — that is the channel's look
  by owner decision).
